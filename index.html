        About FareSplit

        Splits a bus fare based on the segments each passenger actually rode, with exact rounding so shares add up to the total — every cent accounted for.

        • ✅ Ordered stops, custom route
        • ✅ Per-passenger board & alight
        • ✅ Segment-by-segment cost
        • ✅ Remainder absorption (no fractional ₹)
        • ✅ Share via link or QR
        • ✅ Installable PWA · works offline
